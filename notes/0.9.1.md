- **Tooltips you can read.** A chart's tooltip is now solid, in larger type, with a clear border, and says at most three short lines: the figure first in bold, such as "1 / 2 running" or "Failed in 2 s", then what it means or which run, then the time. It sits above or below the chart, never over its labels.
- **The tiles take less room.** Queue, Load and Results lose their card borders and the gaps between them, with a thin line between tiles instead, and the charts run edge to edge. In one column each tile puts its title, its line in words and its number on one row, with the chart right under it.
