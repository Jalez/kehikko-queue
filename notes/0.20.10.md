Kind: feature

- **App updates wait for your queued work.** When Kehikot restarts to update, the restart waits in the queue as its own row, "app restart". Runs ahead of it finish first, nothing queued after it starts, and Kehikot restarts once nothing is running. Cancel the row and Kehikot doesn't restart.
- **Queued work survives the restart.** Runs an agent queued keep their place when Queue restarts. A run a terminal queued is asked for again once Queue is back, instead of running outside the queue.
