- **The tiles read well in one narrow column.** Queue, Load and Results each put their title and number in a row of their own above the chart, so nothing is hidden under the bars, and a tile 260 pixels or wider adds a scale and the first and last time.
- **Charts say their numbers.** Point at a chart, touch it, or Tab to it and use the arrow keys: a small tooltip says the value against its limit and when, such as "1/2 running" with how many more could start, the load against the level where new runs wait, or how long a run took.
